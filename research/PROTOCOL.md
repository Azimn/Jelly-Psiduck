# Research protocol: causal private thought

The broader hypothesis is that an organism-mediated first-person workspace with
recursive thought feedback supports temporal continuity, spontaneous cognition,
relationship-dependent behavior, prospective behavior and self-consistent change
beyond matched prompt-response controls. This hypothesis remains open.

## Initial falsifiable contract

Given the same closed pre-probe organism snapshot and exactly the same thought,
enabling thought-to-state effects must change later appraisal and conduct. The
change must survive restart and must not alter objective world facts.

`python -m jelly_psiduck.evaluation` creates one authored return commitment,
closes a snapshot, and forks five arms before the deadline passes:

| Arm | Intervention |
| --- | --- |
| Full | All currently implemented edges |
| No inner ear | Record thought privately without exposing or processing it again |
| Transcript only | Keep thought and memory context, disable thought-to-state effects |
| No memory feedback | Keep thought re-entry, remove its memory/continuity feedback |
| No autonomous cognition | Advance body/deadline state without unattended thought |

Each arm receives the same fixed thought when cognition is enabled. The harness
checks actual state deltas, selected conduct, next conduct after restart, snapshot
equality on restart, and absence of invented world arrival. Canonical commitment
status is overdue in all arms, preventing a thought from manufacturing resolution.

See [frozen engineering evidence](../evidence/inner-ear-v1.json). The complete loop
selects withdrawal and its controls exploration, with the same distinction after
restart. This establishes wiring for one rule-driven example. It does not establish
that withdrawal is the best behavior or that the architecture improves a real model.

## Methodological limitations and next experiments

- All scenarios, thresholds and checks were designed by the builder. They are
  internal engineering evidence, not independent holdouts or human evaluation.
- The same thought is deliberately fixed. This isolates causal machinery but does
  not measure spontaneous thought quality, language understanding or rumination.
- Current cues use lexical overlap against known tags/actors. Paraphrases may miss
  relevant memories; arbitrary affect inferred from free prose is intentionally absent.
- The full-organism-removal and raw-telemetry-cognition controls are not implemented.
  They require a separate matched interface, not weakening the production firewall.
- Follow with neutral/late/kept/repaired promises, unfamiliar people, conflicting
  testimony, weak recall, long silent periods and lexical distractors; freeze these
  before tuning. Include multiple cartridges and recurrence-budget sensitivity.
- Test grounded prospective proposals through explicit willingness/adoption gates
  before claiming thought-generated commitments.
- Evaluate real models only after freezing prompts, samples and deterministic
  projections. Keep provider failures separate from semantic fidelity failures.
- Define quantitative outcomes and a blinded human protocol before claiming
  recognizability, believability, or comparative benefit over prompt-response agents.

## Negative engineering result retained

The untouched Persona-and-Jelly foundation at the recorded commit failed its own
`test_same_event_produces_different_conduct_after_different_histories`: both arms
selected exploration. A curiosity habit bypassed dominant fear. Restricting the
habit to options from the dominant channel restores the existing test without
introducing another action selector. This defect was reproduced before repair.
